Itineraries and packing lists can be generated by Google's Gemini (2.5 Flash) instead of the built-in generator. The app calls Gemini directly from your browser with your own API key — get a free one at aistudio.google.com/apikey. Your key is stored only on this device. For safety, restrict it to your app's URL under "Edit API key → Website restrictions" in AI Studio, so a copied key can't be used anywhere else. Nothing leaves your browser except requests straight to Google when you tap an "AI regenerate" button.
